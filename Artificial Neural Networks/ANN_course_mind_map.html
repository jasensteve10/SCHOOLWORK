Exam Focus Branches

Task, output, loss mappingStudy ANN_02, ANN_03, and the day 2-4 labs. Watch for sigmoid/softmax vs logits traps.
Training loopStudy ANN_03 and the binary classification labs. Know forward, loss, zero_grad, backward, step.
Underfitting and overfittingStudy ANN_04 and ANN_exercise_curves. Curve interpretation is likely MCQ material.
RegularizationStudy ANN_04 and day 4 labs. Compare L1, L2, dropout, early stopping, batch norm.
Optimizers and gradientsStudy ANN_03 and Optimization_and_Initialization. Know learning rate, momentum, Adam, vanishing gradients, initialization.
PyTorch mechanicsStudy ANN_00 and labs. Know tensors, DataLoader, nn.Module, train/eval mode, TensorBoard, Optuna.
